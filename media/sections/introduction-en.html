Introduction back to ToC

The **BCoreMedia Ontology** extends the Bridge Core Ontology (BCore) with a precise and modular vocabulary for describing **bridge‑related media and documentation**. It models any analog or digital asset from images and videos to inspection reports and sensor recordings, that support the documentation, monitoring, and analysis of a bridge throughout its lifecycle. **BCoreMedia** builds directly on BCore’s top‑level structure, ensuring that every media item can be consistently linked to the bridge, its components, its materials, or its spatial context. It integrates seamlessly with component, material, infrastructure, and zone modules to enable rich, multi‑domain documentation workflows. #### BCore Ontology Suite: | Module | Purpose | |--------|---------| | **[BCore](/BCore/)** | Core identity, top‑level bridge concepts, and shared linking structure. | | **[BCoreComp](/BCore/comp)** | Structural and functional bridge components. | | **[BCoreMat](/BCore/mat)** | Materials used in bridge construction. | | **[BCoreInfra](/BCore/infra)** | Bridge-related transport and utility infrastructure. | | **[BCoreZones](/BCore/zones)** | Spatial zones, geometric context, and environmental regions. | | **BCoreMedia** | Media files, documentation, and digital assets. | #### About BCoreMedia: **BCoreMedia** provides a structured, extensible model for representing the **various types of media assets** relating to a bridge. Main focus: - **Media files** (e. g., images, videos, inspection documents, sensor data etc.) - **Relationships** linking media files to bridges and components - **Consistent integration** with BCore’s core identity and structure - **Interoperability** across all BCore modules → Thus, it is the right choice when you need to capture, organize, and link media assets that support inspection, monitoring, and documentation of a bridge.
