Introduction back to ToC

The **BCoreInfra Ontology** extends the Bridge Core Ontology (BCore) with a precise and modular vocabulary for describing **bridge-related infrastructure**. It models roads, railways, waterways, and other contextual infrastructure elements that interact with or influence the bridge. **BCoreInfra** builds directly on BCore’s top‑level structure, ensuring that every infrastructure element can be consistently linked to the bridge it serves or affects. It integrates seamlessly with component, material, media, and spatial‑zone modules to support comprehensive, multi‑domain bridge modeling. #### BCore Ontology Suite: | Module | Purpose | |--------|---------| | **[BCore](/BCore/)** | Core identity, top‑level bridge concepts, and shared linking structure. | | **[BCoreComp](/BCore/comp)** | Structural and functional bridge components. | | **[BCoreMat](/BCore/mat)** | Materials used in bridge construction. | | **BCoreInfra** | Surrounding transport and utility infrastructure. | | **[BCoreZones](/BCore/zones)** | Spatial zones, geometric context, and environmental regions. | | **[BCoreMedia](/BCore/media)** | Media files, documentation, and digital assets. | #### About BCoreInfra: **BCoreInfra** provides a structured, extensible model for representing the **infrastructure context** of a bridge. Main focus: - **Transport and utility infrastructure** surrounding a bridge - **Clear relationships** between infrastructure elements and the bridge - **Consistent integration** with BCore’s core identity and structure - **Interoperability** with all other BCore modules → Thus, it is the right choice you need to capture how a bridge connects to roads, rail networks, waterways, or other infrastructure.
