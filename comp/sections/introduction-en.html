Introduction back to ToC

The **BCoreComp Ontology** extends the Bridge Core Ontology (BCore) with a precise and modular vocabulary for describing **bridge components**. It focuses on the structural and functional parts that make up a bridge---everything from superstructure and substructure elements to detailed component hierarchies. **BCoreComp** builds directly on BCore’s top‑level structure, ensuring that every component is consistently linked to the bridge and can be combined with other domain modules such as materials, infrastructure, media, and spatial zones. #### BCore Ontology Suite: | Module | Purpose | |--------|---------| | **[BCore](/)** | Core identity, top‑level bridge concepts, and shared linking structure. | | **BCoreComp** | Structural and functional bridge components. | | **[BCoreMat](/mat)** | Materials used in bridge construction. | | **[BCoreInfra](/infra)** | Bridge-related transport and utility infrastructure. | | **[BCoreZones](/zones)** | Spatial zones, geometric context, and environmental regions. | | **[BCoreMedia](/media)** | Media files, documentation, and digital assets. | #### About BCoreComp: **BCoreComp** provides a structured, extensible model for representing the **components that physically constitute a bridge**. Main focus: - **Structural and functional component definitions** - **Clear part-whole hierarchies** within a bridge - **Consistent integration** with BCore’s core identity and structure - **Interoperability** with all other BCore modules → Thus, it is the right choice when you need a clear, precise, and interoperable schema to model the physical composition of a bridge.
